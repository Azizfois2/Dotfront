# Changelog

## v1.1.0

- Android touch build (arm64 + armeabi-v7a) with tap, long-press move, two-finger pan,
  pinch zoom, and on-screen city production/fortify buttons.
- LAN multiplayer: authoritative host, up to five clients, client orders, and AI takeover of
  empty or disconnected factions.
- Mouse-driven menus and overlays with hover feedback, plus clickable city production.
- Native window, taskbar and Dock icon; macOS `.app` bundle with Finder icon.
- Tagged multi-platform releases for Linux, Windows, macOS and Android.
- Version command (`--version`) and in-menu version label.

## v1.0.0

- Offline C RTS with AI opponents, fog of war, organic maps, territory, production queues,
  control groups, save/load, tutorial, LAN-style minimap controls and spectators.
